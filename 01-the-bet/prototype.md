## The Bet in One Sentence
<!-- What you're building, for whom, why now -->
We are building an autonomous research oracle for institutional crypto analysts that converts breaking news and proprietary CoinDesk Index feeds into instantaneous, audit-ready market risk briefs.

## Kill Criteria
<!-- When would you stop? What evidence would kill this bet? -->
If post-launch pilot feedback reveals that institutional analysts reject the AI briefs due to unresolvable hallucinations in regulatory interpretation, OR if time-to-insight exceeds 5 seconds during high-volatility market events, we kill the standalone terminal product and pivot strictly to a headless API data-enrichment layer for existing enterprise systems (e.g., Bloomberg/Refinitiv plug-ins).
